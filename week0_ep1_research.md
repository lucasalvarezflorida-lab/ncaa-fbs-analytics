## 1) North Carolina vs TCU — Dublin, Ireland (Aviva Stadium, noon ET, ESPN)
Aer Lingus Classic, fifth straight Ireland kickoff. Belichick year two opens an ocean from Chapel Hill.

**The number:** DraftKings TCU -6.5 / Bovada -8.5 (opened -7.5), O/U 47.5–49.5.
**Machine:** TCU by 1.5 on the neutral (FPI 6.4 vs 4.9, 54%). Market says a TD+; model says a field goal — 5–7 points of UNC value depending on the book, just under the YEL alert threshold at DK. But respect the skepticism story: UNC's -12.1 residual was the ACC's biggest 2025 overrating by public inputs.

**Stats that decide it:**
- UNC run game was #127 in rush-offense PPA — against TCU's #28 rush defense. If that holds, UNC is one-dimensional all night.
- The counter: TCU's pass defense was #103, and passing is the one thing UNC does (#101 but with a new QB). Edwards vs that secondary is UNC's whole path.
- TCU's pass offense (#29) vs UNC's #49 pass defense is the best-on-best matchup.
- Hidden edge: UNC special teams #31 vs TCU #84.

**Players to watch:**
- UNC: QB **Billy Edwards Jr.** (Maryland grad transfer — 2,881 yds, 15 TD in 2024; Belichick's hand-picked replacement after Gio Lopez left for Wake Forest), RB **Demon June** (Corey's key — but see rush-defense note), WR **Jordan Shipp**, slot **Trech Kekahuna**.
- TCU: QB **Jaden Craig** (grad transfer, first year as the guy after Josh Hoover left for Indiana), WR **Jordan Dwyer**, TE **Ka'Morreun Pimpton**, NT **Markis Deal** anchoring the run front.
- ⚠ Our TCU card's strengths line still references Hoover — stale, don't read it on air.

## 2) NC State at Virginia — Charlottesville (Scott Stadium, 3:30 ET)
Revenge game: NC State won 35-31 last September on an end-zone INT with 1:03 left. Virginia went on to 11 wins and lost the ACC title game to Duke.

**The number:** DraftKings UVA -3 / Bovada -6 (opened -3.5), O/U 53.5.
**Machine:** UVA by 6.7 (69%). Model side is Virginia — and the BOOKS DISAGREE BY A FULL FIELD GOAL, which is a segment in itself. At DK -3 the model sees ~3.7 pts of UVA value; Bovada -6 is priced fair.

**Stats that decide it:**
- UVA is the balance team: #33 rush offense, #42 rush defense, **#26 pass defense** — that last one vs CJ Bailey (#36 pass offense) is the game.
- NC State's ceiling problem: explosiveness bottom-third — they sustain or they stall. UVA's weakness card says turnover-luck regression + senior-driven pass rush gone.
- Special teams gap: UVA #56 vs NCSU **#101** — and our card notes ST cost State two 2025 games. In a 3-point game, this is live.

**Players to watch:**
- UVA: QB **Beau Pribula** (Missouri transfer, named the starter July 19 over Eli Holstein — 67.4% but 11-9 TD-INT; dual-threat, ~300 rush yds/6 TDs), RB **Peyton Lewis**, WR **Rico Flores Jr.**, DE **Fisher Camac**.
- NCSU: QB **CJ Bailey** (year 3 — 3,105 yds, 25 TD at 68.8% in 2025; top-5 returning ACC QB and the league's most returning production), RB **Duke Scott**, WR **JoJo Trader**, DE **Joseph Adedire**.

## 3) Jacksonville State at North Dakota State — Fargodome (5:30 ET, CBSSN)
NDSU's first-ever FBS game; a 2015 FCS-title-game rematch. This one is ON OUR UPSET BOARD.

**The number:** first-seen July 14 at NDSU -10 (our YEL alert, model side Jax State, edge 7.3). Since then: Bovada -7, Fargo media quoting -6.5, O/U 48.
**Machine:** NDSU by ~2.7 (58%) — still on JSU at -7 or worse. The line has already moved 3+ points toward the model's side since first-seen: banked CLV before a snap. Caveats to say out loud: ESPN's preseason FPI on NDSU (-8.3) is a guess with no ratings history — our own card calls it "model blind spot, max uncertainty."

**Stats that decide it:**
- JSU's identity is the #23 rush offense (back-to-back ~3,500-yard rushing seasons) against a Bison front with dynasty polish but zero FBS tape.
- JSU's pass game is #107 — if NDSU stacks the box and makes Creel throw, that's the Bison path.
- JSU special teams #123 — in the Fargodome, hidden-yardage leaks compound.
- NDSU has no 2025 FBS stats by definition: 12-1 FCS, No. 1 seed, stunned at home in the playoffs. CBS set their debut win total at 8.5.

**Players to watch:**
- JSU: QB **Caden Creel** (2,600+ combined yards — and dual-threat QBs have historically been the Bison defense's soft spot, per the local previews), RB **Khristian Lando** (Corey's "unleash" key ✓), DT **Khurtiss Perry**, EDGE **Jacob Cruz**.
- NDSU: QB **Nathan Hayes** — FIRST career start after four years behind NFL QBs; local line: strongest Bison arm since Carson Wentz. Corey's "keep Hayes upright" key is exactly the model's question. RB **DJ Scott**, WR **Mekhi Collins**, and the rebuilt cornerback room (Corey's key — the one unit with no returning proof).
