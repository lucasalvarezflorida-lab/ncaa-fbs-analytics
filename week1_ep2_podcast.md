## Game 1 — Baylor vs Auburn (Atlanta, Sat 3:30 ET)

**The number:** machine **Auburn −5.5** (fair −168/+168, AUB 63%) ·
market **−7 / −7** (DK −298/+240, Bov −270/+220) · O/U 59.5 ·
score: **Auburn 33 – Baylor 27**

**Why the machine favors Auburn:** preseason FPI has Auburn 12.0, Baylor 6.5 —
a 5.5-point gap, no home-field bump (neutral site). The 63% comes from running
+5.5 through the margin curve. Note what that means: the *prior* likes
Auburn's talent base despite the total reboot — it cannot see that Auburn
returns 14% of its production. If install-week chaos is real, this number is
too high on Auburn, which is why the machine sits 1.5 friendlier to Baylor
than the market.

**Why it matters:** Golesh's Auburn debut vs the biggest QB get in Baylor
history (Lagway). Starkest roster contrast on the card — Auburn 14% of
production back / 39 portal adds vs Baylor 60% back. Lagway's health is the
Big 12's biggest single variable. **U-TAIL: lean UNDER the 59.5.** How to say
it on air: *"We audited five years of closing lines and almost everything
checked out — Vegas is good. The one thing that survived: when they hang a
monster total, take the under. Top-decile totals have gone under 55% of the
time for five straight years, and 59.5 is that class of game — with two
year-one offenses in a dome on top."* (Tulsa–Oklahoma State at 61.5 also
qualifies this week, off-card.)

**Contexts:** BAY — Aranda yr 6, new DC Klanderman (K-State); QB NEW Lagway
(Florida); 60% back, 31 adds. AUB — NEW Golesh (USF), Durkin RETAINED as DC;
QB unsettled post-Knight; 14% back, 39 adds.

**Keys — Baylor:** keep Lagway upright; beat Durkin's sim pressures on early
downs; steal a possession with tempo; make Auburn's first-year offense chase.
**Keys — Auburn:** lean on the unchanged defense; tempo without turnovers;
feed the tight ends; let the DL end drives on third down.

## Game 5 — SMU at Florida State (Tallahassee, Mon 7:30 ET) · RED ALERT

**The number:** machine **FSU −0.5** (FSU 52.3%) · market **SMU −3 / −3**
(DK FSU +136/SMU −162 as of Monday night) · O/U 53.5 · score: **FSU 27 – SMU 26**

**WHY WE'RE PREDICTING FSU TO WIN — the full explanation:**
- Preseason FPI: **SMU 11.1, FSU 9.3**. The machine agrees with the market
  that **SMU is the better team** — by 1.8 points on a neutral field.
- The pick flips entirely on the **+2.5 home-field bump** for Monday night at
  the Doak: 9.3 − 11.1 + 2.5 = **FSU +0.7**, booked as −0.5.
- Through the margin curve, +0.7 = **52.3%** — a coin flip leaning FSU, not a
  conviction. The on-air line: *"the machine doesn't think Florida State is
  better — it thinks the game is a pick'em once you give the home team Monday
  night in the Doak. The whole fight with Vegas is whether home field in
  Tallahassee is worth 2.5 points or 5."*
- Why it's flagged RED: the market gives FSU only ~42% (SMU −3, −162). Model
  52% vs market 42% = a 10-point win-prob gap with the model on the dog —
  that's the Upset Board's outright-upset trigger.
- The case FOR the machine: the market may be paying a brand premium on SMU's
  CFP season while FSU won the trenches in the portal (top-10 haul), and 42%
  for a home team the ratings call only 1.8 worse is thin. SMU also lost BOTH
  coordinators (co-coordinator troikas) — coordination risk isn't in anyone's
  preseason number.
- The case AGAINST (say it on air): FSU's prior is the least trustworthy
  number on the card — no program swung harder in 36 months (CFP → 2–10 →
  reset), and preseason FPI is stickiest exactly on brand-name whiplash
  programs. RED alerts as a class backtested **49.7% ATS (2023–25)** — below
  break-even. The flag is a research shortlist, not a pick.

**Why it matters:** Monday night alone on the calendar. FSU whiplash vs SMU's
post-CFP expectations. SMU kept the roster (57% back, 15 adds) but lost the
play-callers; FSU kept Norvell but runs portal-rental QBs again (40% back, 22
adds; Harris promoted to OC after Malzahn retired).

**Keys — SMU:** prove the troika can call it; protect the new QB from the
rebuilt front; explosives, not field goals (White's 3-3-5 bends on purpose);
win takeaways. **Keys — FSU:** run it with the portal front; QB run as the
cheat code; field position over hero ball; start fast, let the Doak matter.
