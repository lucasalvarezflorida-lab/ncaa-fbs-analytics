Man vs Machine · two boards

The Seat and the Trophy

The seat board

Score = 40% what the six human lists say, 60% the model's chance the coach is gone after the season (fit on 2014 to 2025 firings: record against the bar, buyout, tenure, the machine's rating and its move). Projected wins come from the season simulation; the band is the 10th to 90th percentile.

The best-player board

Best so far, not a prediction. Index = points added per game (per-play PPA, shrunk toward last season, times plays per game) × a team factor (half, plus half the chance the team wins ten). All positions on one scale; a receiver sees a dozen plays a game, a quarterback sixty, so the board measures what each one added, not who the voters will pick.

The quarterback index efficiency, every week

The original board: per-play efficiency blended with last season, times the chance of ten wins. Kept beside the all-positions board.
