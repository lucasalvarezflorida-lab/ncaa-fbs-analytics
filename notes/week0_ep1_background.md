(Reported 2026-08-06) — [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/college-football/story/_/id/49545085/north-carolina-dc-steve-belichick-indefinite-medical-leave), [CBS Sports](https://www.cbssports.com/college-football/news/north-carolina-steve-belichick-medical-leave/)

(2026-08-15) — [EssentiallySports](https://www.essentiallysports.com/ncaa-college-football-news-bill-belichick-confirms-unc-has-moved-on-amid-son-steve-belichick-gm-michael-lombardis-absences-things-will-change/)
